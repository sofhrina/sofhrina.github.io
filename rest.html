A small magical inheritance

Slytherin, though the part that stayed longest was never the house competition. It was the idea that books could become doors, that friendship could be an argument against fear, and that ambition needed a choice about what it served.

The HP-shaped cursor is the smallest version of that history: visible if you notice it, harmless if you do not.
