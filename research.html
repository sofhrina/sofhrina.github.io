with a PhD mentor

Robust factor decomposition for hierarchical consumer segmentation

Standard k-means treats each feature as equally informative and returns one flat partition. That is a poor fit for high-dimensional consumer data, where correlated variables can be counted repeatedly and segments may be nested.

I am developing a pipeline that learns factor weights from the data, combines a robust loss with L1 regularisation, and performs two-level clustering. I wrote the abstract, introduction and methodology, and am now turning the mathematical specification into reproducible experiments.

QuestionCan a robust, weighted factor model recover interpretable nested segments?
My partProblem framing, methodology, mathematical writing and experiment design.
NowMethodology drafted; implementation and evaluation pending.

Apr 2026 – present
with a PhD researcher at UCL

Fallacy detection in UK parliamentary debate

This argument-mining project asks how fallacious reasoning can be represented and detected in parliamentary questioning. The scope narrowed from multimodal material to a text-based dataset so that annotation decisions could be made consistently and audited.

I proposed the UK parliamentary setting, helped define a workable fallacy label set, and am building the annotated dataset with argumentation-framework structures and IAT-style graphs. The PhD researcher leads the paper; I contribute to dataset design, annotation and analysis.

OutputA structured parliamentary fallacy dataset and accompanying analysis.
My partSetting selection, label design, annotation protocol and dataset construction.
TargetManuscript in preparation, targeting submission to a leading UK conference.
