Jul 2026 – present · with a PhD mentor

Robust factor decomposition for hierarchical consumer segmentation

Standard k-means struggles with high-dimensional consumer data when correlated features are counted more than once and a single flat partition cannot express nested structure such as spending tier followed by price sensitivity.

I am developing a pipeline that replaces hand-set thresholds, cluster count, and factor weights with parameters learned from the data: a robust loss, learnable factor weights under an L1 penalty, and two-level clustering. I wrote the abstract, introduction, and methodology chapters.

Methodology drafted · experiments not yet run

Apr 2026 – present · with a PhD researcher at UCL

Fallacy detection in UK parliamentary debate

A small team project in argument mining. The work began as multimodal fallacy detection and narrowed over three months to a text-based dataset grounded in parliamentary questioning.

My contributions are proposing the UK parliamentary setting, identifying a workable label set, and building the annotated dataset with argumentation-framework structures and IAT-style argument graphs. The main paper is led and written by the PhD researcher.

Dataset construction · student journal planned for late 2026
